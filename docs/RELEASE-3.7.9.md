# v3.7.9 — Consolidated commerce integrations

- Remove the standalone EZPAY and ECPay invoice modules, cards, settings routes, adapters and their private vendor runtimes. They are no longer shipped or loaded even if their old activation flags remain in a site's options. Their source remains recoverable in Git history / v3.7.8.
- Retain `ecpay-tools` and its `wu-ecpay-tools` Toolbox entry, including the original ECPay payment, logistics and electronic invoice implementation. No files within that module are changed. Merchant credentials, service switches, callbacks and existing official ECPay order metadata remain unchanged.
- Rename the combined commerce integration group to **電商第三方金物流**. PAYUNi, LINE Pay and ECPay remain in this group between 電商工具 and 電商優化工具. Dashboard counts, quick navigation and grouped submenus derive from the same registry.
- No database deletion, invoice issuing/voiding, provider conversion or automatic ECPay activation occurs. Retired standalone options and order records remain stored. They are not equivalent to official ECPay records and are deliberately not auto-imported; merchants who previously used the standalone modules must confirm provider records before operating through a different integration.
- Regression checks cover stale enabled flags for retired modules, retained merchant/legacy/order-data fixtures, provider source checksums, early loading and disabled loading, original settings entry/capability guard, category order and browser/mobile quick navigation. Existing complete validation remains enabled, replacing tests specific to removed runtimes with consolidation coverage.
- No production site is installed or real payment, shipment or invoice operation performed by these checks. Verify merchant settings and test-environment operations before live use.
